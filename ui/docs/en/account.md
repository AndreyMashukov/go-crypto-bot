#### Why is an internal balance necessary?
The internal balance is necessary to pay for the service; we spend significant resources on development, support, and server rental, as well as maintaining the entire infrastructure of the project for its stable and uninterrupted operation. **We do not have access to your funds on the cryptocurrency exchanges; the bot trades strictly within the limits of the exchange by buying and selling a particular cryptocurrency.**

> [!NOTE]
> **Service Payment Options:**
> * **Commission:** Deduction from the internal balance (budget) of the Autotrade.cloud system, with a commission ranging from 25-50% of the profit earned after closing a position (see the commission table below).
> * **Subscription:** Purchasing a subscription for 890 USDT exempts the user (trader) from paying the commission.

| Level | Monthly Trading Volume      | Commission Rate | Minimum Commission |
|-------|-----------------------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| 1     | 0.00 USDT - 5000.00 USDT    | 50.00%          | 1.00$              |
| 2     | 5000.00 USDT - 25000.00 USDT| 35.00%          | 1.00$              |
| 3     | 25000.00 USDT - 50000.00 USDT| 30.00%         | 1.00$              |
| 4     | > 50000.00 USDT             | 25.00%          | 1.00$              |

> [!warning]
> Trading volume is calculated separately for each bot, and the commission percentage for each bot is determined based on the monthly trading volume of that specific bot.

> [!warning]
> The commission cannot be less than the **minimum commission**, which is **1.00$** (one dollar). Please consider this when setting the budget and the percentage of minimum profit.

#### Setting up the trading bot
After registration in the personal account, a user can create and configure bots; currently, there are two bots available:
* Bot for Binance
* Bot for ByBit

To configure the bot for your exchange, click on `SET UP`, and a dialog box will open where you need to enter the API key and API secret, which you can find in your account on Binance or ByBit.
