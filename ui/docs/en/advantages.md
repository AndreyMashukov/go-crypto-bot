### Six Clear Advantages of the Autotrade.cloud Platform Compared to Other Bots

1. **Open Source Code**: The source code of our trading bot is completely open and available on [GitHub](http://github.com/AndreyMashukov/go-crypto-bot). This ensures not only full transparency of our operations but also an additional guarantee of security, allowing users to independently verify and audit our algorithms.

2. **[Flexible Trading Strategy Configuration](/en/dashboard.md)**: Autotrade.cloud users can finely tune trading strategies, selecting parameters for buying, selling, and averaging, giving them the ability to customize their trading to fit their needs.

3. **[Management of Bots from One Platform](/en/account.md)**: The platform allows managing bots for various exchanges such as Binance and ByBit from a single interface, simplifying portfolio management and optimizing trading operations.

4. **Protection Against Loss-Making Transactions**: The built-in logic of the bots on Autotrade.cloud prohibits selling cryptocurrency at a loss. The bot is configured so that sales can only be made with a minimum profit of 0.5%, preventing unprofitable transactions.

5. **[AI Signals](/en/signal.md) for Smart Trading**: The platform uses advanced AI signals to generate trading recommendations, which include information on average buying and selling prices, providing reliable benchmarks for trading, especially for beginners.

6. **Careful Selection of Cryptocurrencies**: Only top cryptocurrencies by market capitalization, selected based on data from [CoinMarketCap](https://www.coinmarketcap.com), are available on Autotrade.cloud. This ensures high quality of trading assets and enhances investment security.
