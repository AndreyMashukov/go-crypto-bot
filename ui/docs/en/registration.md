# Registration

> [!Note]
> **Ready to trade?** Go to [autotrade.cloud](https://autotrade.cloud)

<img src="/en/img/register/2.png" alt="Registration icon" width="100%"/>

Registration on the platform is done via `email`, to do this go to the homepage and click on the icon <img src="/img/register/1.png" alt="Registration icon" width="25px"/>. A modal window will open where you will need to enter your `nickname` (your name on the platform, use only English characters from a to z and numbers from 0 to 9) and `email`. If you do not have a promo code, leave the `promo code` field empty. Click the `GET CODE TO MAIL` button.

<img src="/en/img/register/3.png" alt="Registration icon" width="100%" style="max-width: 500px;"/>

After you have requested the code by email, check your email, and also remember to check the `SPAM` folder <img src="/img/register/5.png" alt="SPAM folder" height="35px"/>. The email will contain a code.

<img src="/en/img/register/6.png" alt="Registration icon" width="100%" style="max-width: 500px;"/>

You will need to enter this code in the next tab of the registration window in the `code from email` field, `email` should be auto-filled from the previous step. After entering this information, press `LOGIN`. If the data is correct, you will be redirected to your personal account.

<img src="/en/img/register/4.png" alt="Registration icon" width="100%" style="max-width: 500px;"/>
