# Trading AI Signals

> [!Note]
> **Ready to trade?** Go to [autotrade.cloud](https://autotrade.cloud)

Trading AI signals are generated by a neural network, which is one of the modules of the [Autotrade.cloud](https://autotrade.cloud) platform. We cannot disclose all the details of how this neural network works, but the result of its generation is a **"signal" - a ready configuration of a decision to buy and sell cryptocurrency, containing purchase prices, a stepped configuration of selling prices, and one step of averaging**. The neural network is trained and makes decisions based on [PIVOT points](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/pivotpoint.asp) and market indicators (order book, historical data for the last 2 days).

### Signal Correction and Filtering (Signal Configuration)

The system provides the ability for users to adjust trading AI signals, changing their buy and sell prices and setting a minimum signal profit percentage. This can be done by clicking on the icon <img src="/img/signal/3.png" alt="ability to adjust trading AI signals by users" width="25px"/> next to the signal trading switch in the trading stack.

<img src="/en/img/signal/4.png" alt="Trading AI signals and their user settings" width="100%" style="max-width: 750px;margin-top: 10px;margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

When we open the settings, we see that `LINKUSDT` has 20 successfully closed deals and the average order prices are: **Average Buy** 15.43941 USDT **Average Sell** 15.65722 USDT. Users can adjust signals using the switches in the dialog box.

* `Skip if signal percent less than` - Signals with a percentage less than the specified value will be ignored and will not be passed to the trading bot
* `Use Average BUY price` - Enables the adjustment of the buy price in the signal, taking the lower value between the signal buy price and the average buy price from the rating
* `Sell price correction mode` - There are three options: **`Max value (AVG or Signal)`** **Will take the higher value between the signal sell price and the average sell price from the rating**, **`Min value (AVG or Signal)`** **Will take the lower value between the signal sell price and the average sell price from the rating**, **`Equal Average Sell`** **Will take the value of the average sell price from the rating**,
* `Use Average SELL price` - Enables the adjustment of the sell price in the signal, logic depends on the `Sell Price Adjustment Mode`
* `Must Have Rating` - If enabled, signals for cryptocurrencies not in the rating will be ignored
* `Skip if BUY price greater than Average` - If enabled, signals where the buy price is greater than the average buy price in the rating will be ignored (but if there is no rating and the `Must Have Rating` flag is not enabled, the signals will be passed to the bot, as there is no rating - no value)
* `Skip if SELL price greater than Average` - If enabled, signals where the sell price is greater than the average sell price in the rating will be ignored (but if there is no rating and the `Must Have Rating` flag is not enabled, the signals will be passed to the bot, as there is no rating - no value)
